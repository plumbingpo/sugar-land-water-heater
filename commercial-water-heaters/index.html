How to Tell Something Is Wrong

Most tanks announce trouble weeks or months ahead with rumbling noises, rusty water, or hot showers that keep getting shorter. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • An aging unit you would rather replace on your schedule than in an emergency
  • Distant fixtures that take minutes to run warm
  • Higher utility bills that point to a heater losing efficiency
  • Visible corrosion or water pooling near the tank
  • Fixtures that run too hot one minute and cool the next
  • Rumbling, popping, or banging from a heavily used tank
  • Lukewarm water at the exact time you need the most
  • Water temperatures that worry you before a health inspection

Tips to Keep Your Water Heater Healthy

A few preventive steps can help you avoid a cold shower or a wet floor down the road. Here are our recommendations:

  • Leave space around commercial heaters for airflow and safe servicing
  • Flush commercial tanks more often than residential ones, often every few months in heavy use or hard water
  • Test thermostatic mixing valves periodically to confirm safe delivery temperatures
  • Keep recirculation pumps and check valves in service to avoid long waits and wasted energy
  • Track temperatures at the dish machine and hand sinks as part of routine checks
  • Check anodes on a schedule matched to your volume and water quality
  • Budget for replacement as equipment nears the end of its expected life
